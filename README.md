### 👋 About Me

I’m a software engineer working on building and maintaining **reliable, production-ready systems** used in real-world environments.

Over time, I’ve learned that good engineering goes far beyond writing working code. It’s about **clarity, structure, security, and long-term maintainability**—and about making decisions that help teams move forward without creating future friction.

I enjoy working on backend-heavy systems, understanding business requirements deeply, and translating them into solutions that are stable, readable, and easy to evolve. I’m constantly learning—through production challenges, code reviews, and discussions with peers and seniors—and I value thoughtful engineering over quick wins.

---

### 🔍 What I Focus On

- Building backend services that support real business workflows  
- Designing systems that remain maintainable as they grow  
- Applying security and access control as first-class concerns  
- Structuring applications so teams can work comfortably and confidently  
- Preparing applications for deployment, monitoring, and long-term stability  
- Learning continuously from real-world failures and improvements  

I believe understanding *why* something is built is just as important as *how* it’s built.
